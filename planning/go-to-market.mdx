# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP: Mid-Market SaaS Engineering Teams

**Company Profile**:
- 30-200 engineers (sweet spot: 50-100)
- Series A-C funded (or bootstrapped, profitable)
- Building software with complex requirements
- 10+ projects in active development
- Remote or hybrid team
- Located: North America (US/Canada), Europe

**Decision Makers**:
- VP of Engineering / Engineering Director (primary)
- CTO / Head of Quality (secondary)
- Tech Lead / Principal Engineer (influencer)

**Pain Points**:
- "We can't see how code relates to requirements"
- "Change impact is unknown until production"
- "Testing coverage is spotty and invisible"
- "Onboarding engineers takes too long"
- "We keep discovering dependencies the hard way"

**Budget**: $2,000-10,000/month for software tools
- Comparison: Jira ($2k), GitHub Enterprise ($5k), DataDog ($5k+)
- Traceability ROI: 5x typical implementation timeline savings

**Buying Signal**: Started traceability initiative and realized it's too manual

### Secondary ICP: Enterprise Organizations

**Company Profile**:
- 500+ engineers
- Compliance-heavy (finance, healthcare, defense)
- Multiple teams/divisions
- High change management overhead
- Self-hosted infrastructure requirement

**Decision Makers**:
- CTO
- VP Engineering
- Compliance/Risk Officer
- Enterprise Architect

**Pain Points**:
- Compliance audits require 2-3 weeks of documentation
- Change control is manual and slow
- Can't prove test coverage for regulatory reasons
- Integration with internal tools (Jira, Azure DevOps, Jenkins)

**Budget**: $50,000+/year for mission-critical tools

**Buying Signal**: Regulatory audit revealed traceability gaps

## Customer Acquisition Strategy

### Phase 1: Design Partner Validation (Months 1-6)

**Goal**: Find 5-10 design partners, validate problem-solution fit

**Approach**:
1. **Identify Target Companies**
   - Use LinkedIn, Crunchbase, AngelList to find Series A-B SaaS companies
   - Filter: 30-100 engineers, raising or profitable
   - Target list: 100+ prospects

2. **Reach Out (Week 1-4)**
   - Personalized email to VP Engineering
   - Subject: "One question about your engineering team"
   - Pitch: Free access + influence product + early customer discount
   - Target response rate: 10-20% (10 positive conversations)

3. **Screening Conversation (Week 2-6)**
   - 30-minute call with VP Engineering
   - Validate problem: "How do you manage requirement → code → test traceability?"
   - Look for: Pain level, team size, budget, implementation timeline
   - Select: 5-10 who show strong problem indication

4. **Onboard Design Partners (Week 4-8)**
   - Dedicated Slack channel for partner
   - Weekly 1:1s (30 min)
   - Office hours (2x per week, optional)
   - Monthly all-hands (all partners + team)
   - Monthly feedback survey (NPS, feature requests)

5. **Pricing**:
   - Cost to partner: $500-1,000/month (50% off Professional)
   - Benefit to partner: Direct influence, free implementation, case study
   - Commitment: 6 months

**Success Metrics**:
- 5-10 design partners by M4
- 80%+ usage (at least 2 logins per week)
- Actionable feedback (10+ features requested)
- NPS ≥ 50
- 3+ customer testimonials
- 1-2 case studies for marketing

### Phase 3: Sales & Enterprise (Months 13-18+)

**Goal**: Build repeatable enterprise sales process, reach $120k+ MRR

**Sales Strategy**:

#### Sales Team
- **Month 15**: Hire VP of Sales
- **Month 16**: Hire 2-3 Account Executives (focus: enterprise)
- **Month 18**: Add Customer Success Manager

#### Sales Process for Enterprise
1. **Outbound prospecting** (LinkedIn, email, events)
2. **Discovery call** (30 min) - understand pain, budget, timeline
3. **Product demo** (45 min) - show how we solve their specific pain
4. **Pilot program** (30 days) - limited team, free/discounted
5. **Evaluation & negotiation** (2 weeks) - compare vs. alternatives
6. **Close** (contract signing)
7. **Implementation** (4-8 weeks) - onboarding, training, customization

**Sales Cycle**: 2-4 months average

#### Account-Based Marketing (ABM)
- Identify 20-30 target enterprise accounts
- Customize messaging for each
- Multi-touch outreach (email, LinkedIn, event)
- Executive engagement

**Expected Results**:
- 1-2 enterprise customers per month (starting M15)
- 5-10 enterprise customers total by M18
- $5,000-20,000 contract value (average)
- Total enterprise MRR: $30k-50k by M18

**Problem**: "My team wastes hours on impact analysis and can't see how code relates to requirements"  
**Solution**: "AverisOS connects requirements → code → tests → releases so impact is instant"  
**Benefit**: "Ships faster, catches bugs earlier, onboarding takes days not weeks"

#### For CTO/Architects
**Problem**: "Our architecture has hidden dependencies and complex relationships we can't visualize"  
**Solution**: "Complete traceability graph with impact analysis"  
**Benefit**: "Make better architectural decisions with full visibility"

#### For QA/Testing
**Problem**: "We don't know which code is tested and which requirements lack tests"
