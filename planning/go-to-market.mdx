# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Customer Profile (ICP)

### Ideal Customer
- **Company Size:** Mid-market SaaS (200-2000 employees)
- **Engineering Headcount:** 50-500 engineers
- **Team Structure:** 3-10 engineering teams
- **Current Tools:** Using 15+ tools (Jira, GitHub, Confluence, Slack, etc.)
- **Pain Points:** Tool fatigue, context loss, slow releases
- **Budget:** $2,000-10,000/month for better tooling
- **Buying Motive:** "We spend more time managing tools than shipping features"

## Phase 1: Design Partner Program (Months 1-3)

**Goal:** Validate product-market fit with 5-10 beta users

### Recruitment
- Target: Warm introductions (your network, advisors)
- Selection: Diverse industries, team sizes, use cases
- Value prop: "Free access + weekly feedback sessions + case study"

### Engagement
- Weekly 1:1 calls (30 min)
- Monthly feedback reviews (1 hour)
- Access to founders for questions
- Monthly updates on roadmap

### Deliverables
- 3-5 case studies
- Testimonials for launch
- Feature validation data
- User behavior insights

### Success Metrics
- 5-10 design partners signed
- Weekly active usage (>80%)
- Strong qualitative feedback (NPS > 50)
- 2-3 ready to convert to paying

## Phase 2: Public Launch & Growth (Months 4-8)

### Content & Thought Leadership

**Blog Posts (Monthly)**
1. "The Cost of Tool Fragmentation" ($100k/year waste study)
2. "Building a Traceability-First Engineering System"
3. "DORA Metrics: How to Measure Engineering Productivity"
4. "From Chaos to Connected: One Team's Story"

**Whitepaper**
- "Engineering Operating Systems: The Future of Software Delivery"
- Download gate: Email capture
- Distribution: LinkedIn, Hacker News, Twitter

**LinkedIn Strategy**
- 2-3 posts per week (insights on software delivery)
- Engage in engineering communities
- Comment on relevant discussions

### Product Hunt Launch (Month 4-5)

**Preparation**
- Gather design partner testimonials
- Create launch video (5 min demo)
- Write compelling product description
- Line up expert hunters

**Launch Day**
- Target: Top 10-20 products
- Goal: 1,000+ upvotes
- Engagement: Active in comments all day
- Press: Send press release

**Post-Launch**
- Convert PH users to trial
- Free tier for community
- Collect feedback

### Sales Outreach (Ongoing)

**Cold Outreach**
- Target: VP Eng, Engineering Leaders at ICP companies
- Message: Problem relevance → product fit
- Channel: LinkedIn, email, warm intros
- Cadence: 2-3 outreaches per week

**Sales Process**
1. Discovery call (30 min): Understand pain points
2. Product demo (30 min): Show core value
3. Trial period (1-2 weeks): Free access, guided setup
4. Close (1-2 weeks): Negotiate terms, onboard

**Sales Cycle:** 30-60 days for mid-market

### Partnerships (Months 6-8)

**GitHub/GitLab Marketplace**
- List in integrations
- Co-marketing opportunity
- Drive referral traffic

**Jira Marketplace**
- Jira app integration
- Cross-promotion

**Consulting Partners**
- Systems integrators who recommend tools
- Joint go-to-market

**Community Groups**
- YPO (Young Presidents' Organization)
- CTO Collective
- VP Eng networks

## Phase 3: Enterprise Sales & Scale (Months 9-18)

### Sales Team
- Hire VP Sales (Month 8-9)
- Hire 2-3 Account Executives (Month 10-12)
- Hire Sales Development Reps (Month 12+)

### Enterprise Sales Strategy
- Longer sales cycles (60-90 days)
- Multiple stakeholders (VP Eng, CTO, CISO)
- Requirements: SLA, security, compliance
- Deal size: $5-50k/month

### Brand Building
- Speaking engagements at DevOps Days, QCon, etc.
- Industry reports and benchmarks
- Thought leadership positioning
- Community events

## Messaging Framework

### Headline
> "The engineering operating system that connects your entire software lifecycle"

### Tagline
> "From customer need to production confidence"

### Key Messages

**For Engineers**
- Work in your IDE and CLI
- Understand the full impact of changes
- No more context switching between tools

**For Engineering Leaders**
- Know your delivery health
- Release with confidence
- Make data-driven decisions

**For Product Managers**
- Understand which features matter
- Track from research to release
- Measure customer impact

### Story Arc
1. **Problem:** Tool fragmentation wastes $100k+/year per company
2. **Vision:** One platform for the entire software lifecycle
3. **Solution:** Connected artifacts through a knowledge graph
4. **Results:** Faster releases, higher confidence, better decisions
