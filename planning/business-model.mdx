# AverisOS Business Model

## Value Proposition

AverisOS solves critical problems for software teams:

| Problem | Cost of Not Solving | AverisOS Solution |
|---------|-------------------|------------------|
| Hidden dependencies | Requirements break silently | Complete traceability graph |
| Manual impact analysis | Days to assess changes | Instant impact visualization |
| Test coverage gaps | 30%+ of code untested | Automated gap detection |
| Change risk | Costly surprises in production | Quantified risk scoring |
| Compliance audits | Weeks of manual documentation | Automatic audit trail |

**Economic impact**: Teams save 10-20 hours/month on manual traceability work, prevent 2-3 production incidents/year from change blindness.

## Pricing Strategy

### SaaS Tiered Pricing

AverisOS uses value-based pricing with three main tiers:

#### Starter Tier
**$500/month** (annual: $5,400)

For teams getting started with traceability:
- Up to 5 projects
- Up to 10 team members
- 100K artifacts
- Basic impact analysis
- Community support
- Self-service onboarding
- **Ideal customer**: Startup, proof-of-concept team

#### Professional Tier
**$2,000/month** (annual: $21,600)

For teams with mature traceability needs:
- Unlimited projects
- Unlimited team members
- 10M artifacts
- Advanced impact analysis
- Coverage detection
- AI-powered link suggestions
- Open-source CLI included
- Priority email support
- **Ideal customer**: Mid-market SaaS, agency with 30-100 engineers

#### Enterprise Tier
**Custom pricing** ($5,000+ per month)

For large organizations with custom needs:
- Unlimited everything
- Self-hosted or private cloud deployment
- SSO/SAML, LDAP integration
- Custom integrations
- Advanced audit & compliance
- Dedicated support (Slack, phone)
- Custom SLAs
- Volume discounts (20+ licenses)
- **Ideal customer**: Enterprise with 100+ engineers, compliance requirements

### Self-Hosted Licensing

Separate from SaaS offering:

**Self-Hosted Base License**: $10,000/year
- Unlimited artifacts, projects, team members
- Everything in Professional tier features
- Docker/Kubernetes deployment
- Updates & security patches
- Community support

**Self-Hosted Premium**: $25,000+/year
- Everything in base license
- Dedicated support
- Custom integrations
- Advanced compliance features (HIPAA, SOC 2)
- Priority feature requests

**Self-Hosted Enterprise**: Custom pricing
- Everything in premium
- Guaranteed support response times (1 hour)
- On-site training & onboarding
- Custom development

## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

#### Startup Phase (Phase 1)
- Design partner program (no CAC, pre-revenue)
- Early adopter focus (organic + content marketing)
- Estimated CAC: $500-1000 (low because organic)
- CAC payback period: 1-2 months

#### Growth Phase (Phase 2-3)
- Paid channels: content marketing, events, ads
- Sales team for enterprise
- Estimated CAC: $5,000-15,000
- CAC payback period: 3-6 months for Professional tier

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

#### Starter Tier
- Annual revenue: $500 × 12 = $6,000
- Churn rate: 20% (12-month lifetime)
- LTV: $6,000 × 5 years average × (1 - 0.2) = $24,000

#### Professional Tier
- Annual revenue: $2,000 × 12 = $24,000
- Churn rate: 5% (longer lifetime, high stickiness)
- LTV: $24,000 × 10 years average × (1 - 0.05) = $228,000

#### Enterprise Tier
- Annual revenue: $50,000-200,000+ (custom)
- Churn rate: <1% (high switching costs)
- LTV: $100,000 × 10+ years × 0.99 = $1,000,000+

**Average LTV: $200,000-300,000** (mix of tiers)

### LTV:CAC Ratio

- **Phase 1 (design partners)**: LTV:CAC = ∞ (no CAC)
- **Phase 2 (growth)**: LTV:CAC = 30:1 to 50:1 (healthy)
- **Phase 3 (maturity)**: LTV:CAC = 50:1+ (excellent)

Rule of thumb: LTV:CAC > 3:1 is sustainable, > 5:1 is excellent.

## Pricing Justification

### Why These Price Points?

#### Starter at $500/month
- **Competitive**: Similar to GitHub, Linear (issue tracking)
- **Accessible**: Affordable for startups, side projects
- **Viable**: Covers some infrastructure + support costs
- **Upsell path**: Easy to upgrade to Professional as team grows

#### Professional at $2,000/month
- **Value-based**: Teams save 10-20 hrs/month ([$100-200 per hour](methodology))
- **Competitive**: Similar to Jira enterprise add-ons
- **Profitable**: Covers engineering + support + infrastructure
- **Volume** Growth from Starter tier

#### Enterprise (custom)
- **Complex needs**: Custom integrations, deployment, compliance
- **Relationship-driven**: Requires sales, legal, support
- **High LTV**: Multi-year contracts, low churn
- **Negotiated**: Based on company size, deployment, features

### Why SaaS + Self-Hosted?

**SaaS advantages**:
- Lower barrier to entry (no infrastructure)
- Recurring revenue
- Automatic updates
- Easier to sell to teams

**Self-hosted advantages**:
- High-margin contracts
- Attracts enterprises (data residency, compliance)
- Premium pricing possible
- Diverse revenue stream

## Go-to-Market Revenue

### Phase 1: Design Partner Revenue

**Design Partner Program Structure**:
- 5-10 design partners selected
- Special pricing: 50% off Professional tier
- Cost to partner: $1,000/month ($500 discount)
- Benefit to partner: Influence product direction, priority support
- Timeline: M4-6

**Phase 1 Revenue Projection**:
- 8 design partners × $1,000/month = $8,000 MRR
- Annual revenue: $96,000
- Purpose: Validate product-market fit, fund team through Phase 1

### Phase 2: Market Launch

**Launch Channels**:
1. **Content Marketing** (60% of new customers)
   - Blog posts (1-2 per week)
   - YouTube tutorials
   - Industry guides
   - Case studies from design partners

2. **Product Hunt** (20% of new customers)
   - #1 product launch goal
   - Early adopter audience
   - Organic signups

3. **Community** (20% of new customers)
   - GitHub discussions
   - Discord community
   - Hacker News, Dev.to
   - Email newsletter

**Phase 2 Revenue Projection**:
- M7: $5k MRR (design partners + early adopters)
- M8: $8k MRR
- M9: $12k MRR
- M10: $18k MRR (growing sales team)
- M11: $25k MRR
- M12: $38k MRR
- Total Phase 2 revenue: $145k

### Phase 3: Scaling

**Enterprise Sales**:
- Hire dedicated sales team (1-2 reps)
- Target mid-market (100-500 engineers)
- Contract size: $30k-100k+ annually
- Sales cycle: 2-4 months

**Phase 3 Revenue Projection**:
- Enterprise contracts start closing M15+
- $100k+ monthly revenue by end of year 2
