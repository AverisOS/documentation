# Business Model & Pricing

## Pricing Tiers

### Starter: $500/month
**For:** Startups, small teams, open-source projects

- Up to 10 users
- 1 project
- 100k artifacts
- Community support (Slack/Discord)
- Basic features (artifact management, traceability, CLI)

### Professional: $2,000/month
**For:** Mid-market teams, Series A-B companies

- Up to 50 users
- 5 projects
- Unlimited artifacts
- Email support (48h response)
- All AI agents
- Custom skills
- Advanced integrations

### Enterprise: Custom Pricing
**For:** Large organizations, compliance requirements

- Unlimited users & projects
- SLA (99.5% uptime)
- Dedicated support (24h response)
- Advanced RBAC & audit logs
- Custom integrations
- On-premise or private cloud option
- Training & onboarding

### Open-Source: Free
**For:** Public open-source projects

- Full feature access
- Community support
- Logo on website

## Revenue Model

- **Subscription-based** (primary): Monthly/annual contracts
- **Annual discount**: 10% off annual prepay
- **Add-ons**: Premium support, custom integrations
- **Usage-based** (optional): $0.01 per AI agent run above limit
- **Professional services**: Implementation, custom development ($200-300/hr)

## Financial Projections

### Year 1: Validation Phase
- **Customers:** 10-20
- **MRR:** $10-30k
- **ARR:** $120-360k
- **Focus:** Design partners, early adopters, case studies
- **Spending:** Team (salaries), infrastructure, marketing

### Year 2: Growth Phase
- **Customers:** 50-100
- **MRR:** $100-200k
- **ARR:** $1.2-2.4M
- **Focus:** Expansion, product-market fit signals
- **Spending:** Sales, marketing, engineering

### Year 3: Scale Phase
- **Customers:** 200-300
- **MRR:** $300-500k
- **ARR:** $3.6-6M
- **Focus:** Enterprise customers, market leadership
- **Spending:** Enterprise sales, brand building, new domains

## Unit Economics (Target)

- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** $5,000
- **Payback Period:** 3-4 months
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** $120k-300k
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** 24x (target: >3x is healthy, >5x is excellent)
- **Magic Number:** (MRR growth) / Sales & Marketing = 0.5-1.0

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Design Partners (Months 1-3)
- 5-10 carefully selected beta users
- Free/discounted access in exchange for feedback
- Weekly 1:1 calls
- Goal: 3-5 case studies

### Phase 2: Public Launch (Months 4-8)
- Product Hunt launch
- Content marketing (blog, white papers)
- Cold outreach to ICP
- LinkedIn prospecting
- Goal: 20+ inbound leads, 10-20 customers

### Phase 3: Enterprise Sales (Months 9-18)
- Dedicated sales team
- Target enterprise accounts
- Partnerships with systems integrators
- Conference speaking
- Goal: Enterprise customers, multi-year contracts
