# ADR-009: Design Partner Program Over Public Beta

**Date**: September 2026  
**Status**: ✅ Accepted  
**Author**: Aaron

---

## Context

How should we validate the AverisOS MVP? Public beta or closed design partner program?

### Public Beta
- Open to anyone
- Lots of feedback
- Viral potential

### Design Partner Program
- 5-10 carefully selected customers
- Deep relationship
- Actionable feedback

---

## Decision

**Design Partner Program**:

- Target 5-10 carefully selected companies in Phase 1
- Weekly 1:1 check-ins with each partner
- Feedback directly influences Phase 2 roadmap
- Convert design partners to paying customers early

---

## Alternatives Considered

### 1. Public Beta
**Pros**: Large audience, viral potential, lots of feedback  
**Cons**: Noisy feedback, hard to prioritize, surface-level insights

**Verdict**: Too chaotic for MVP learning.

### 2. Design Partner Program (This Decision)
**Pros**: Deep feedback, validation, early revenue, relationships  
**Cons**: Smaller sample size, might miss broader market signals

**Verdict**: Right balance.

### 3. Stealth (No external feedback)
**Pros**: Perfect product before sharing  
**Cons**: Miss market signals, slow validation, risk building wrong thing

**Verdict**: Too risky.

---

## Rationale

### ✓ Quality Feedback
Design partners give **deep, actionable feedback**:
- Why they use (or don't use) features
- How they integrate into workflows
- What's blocking adoption
- What competitive features matter

Public beta gives:
- "Doesn't work" with no context
- Feature requests without understanding why
- Low engagement (most don't come back)

### ✓ Validation
Design partners are **early customers**:
- Willing to pay (or commit to paying)
- Serious about solving problem
- Will give honest feedback
- Will champion product to others

Public beta users are:
- Free riders
- May not need problem solved
- No commitment
- Churn is high

### ✓ Revenue
Convert design partners to paying customers early:
- Already invested in success
- Know the product works for them
- Easier sales conversation
- Faster path to revenue

### ✓ Relationships
Build **relationships that scale**:
- Design partners become customers
- Customers become advocates
- Advocates refer more customers
- Each relationship is deep, not broad

### ✓ Focus
Work with 5-10 companies means:
- Know their pain points intimately
- Understand their workflows
- Can customize features
- Can support them properly

---

## Consequences

### ✅ Positive
- **High-quality feedback**: Actionable, deep insights
- **Fast validation**: Know if product works in weeks, not months
- **Early revenue**: Design partners convert faster
- **Better roadmap**: Build what customers actually need
- **Relationships**: Turn partners into advocates

### ⚠️ Negative
- **Limited sample size**: Might miss broader market signals
- **Selection bias**: Choose partners similar to us
- **Slower growth**: Not hundreds of users testing
- **Higher touch**: Need time for 1:1s
- **Risk**: What if all partners are similar to each other?

### 🛡️ Mitigation
- **Diverse selection**: Choose partners from different industries/company sizes
- **Regular customer interviews**: Talk to non-partners too
- **Quantitative metrics**: Track usage data alongside feedback
- **Public launch plan**: Transition to public beta in Phase 2

---

## Design Partner Selection

### Ideal Design Partner Profile
- ✓ Uses our target platform (GitHub, GitLab, Jira, etc.)
- ✓ Team size 10-100 people (not too small, not massive enterprises)
- ✓ Tech-forward company
- ✓ Values quality over speed
- ✓ Willing to give regular feedback
- ✓ Willing to be case study (eventually)
- ✓ Non-competing (not building similar product)

### Industries to Target (Phase 1)
1. **B2B SaaS companies**
   - Already use GitHub, Jira, CI/CD
   - Care about delivery speed
   - Have clear feature-to-requirements traceability needs

2. **Fortune 500 software teams**
   - Compliance/audit requirements
   - Large teams (need artifacts)
   - Budget for tools

3. **Open-source projects**
   - Community-driven
   - Issues + PRs already exist
   - Natural traceability need

### Recruitment Channels
1. **Warm introductions** (through founders' network)
2. **Industry conferences** (find leads, invite to try)
3. **LinkedIn outreach** (to engineering leads)
4. **Community partnerships** (open-source foundations)
5. **Content marketing** (write about traceability problem)

## Design Partner Program Structure

### Phase 1A: Recruitment (Months 1-2)
- Target 5-10 companies
- 1-hour initial conversation
- Sign NDA + design partner agreement
- Set up shared Slack channel

### Phase 1B: Development (Months 1-3)
- **Weekly 1:1s**: 30 min syncs with each partner
- **Office hours**: Open Slack/Zoom for questions
- **Monthly all-hands**: All partners together for roadmap discussion
- **Early access**: Partners use beta software before public
- **Bug bounty program**: Small rewards for bug reports

### Phase 1C: Transition (Month 3)
- **Roadmap feedback**: What do they want in Phase 2?
- **Pricing feedback**: What would they pay?
- **Case study**: Can we feature them?
- **Convert to customers**: Transition from partner to paying customer

## Design Partner Agreement

```
DESIGN PARTNER AGREEMENT

1. Confidentiality
   - Keep AverisOS roadmap confidential
   - No public discussions until Phase 2 launch

2. Regular Feedback
   - Weekly 30-min sync calls
   - Monthly written feedback

3. Honesty
   - Tell us what's working and what's not
   - No polite feedback; we need honest critique

4. Case Study (Optional)
   - May feature in marketing
   - Your team name and logo (public)
   - Story of how AverisOS helped (you review)

5. Transition
   - At end of Phase 1, become customer or graduate program
   - No obligation to pay, but we'll offer special pricing

6. Mutual NDA
   - Protect both parties' confidentiality
```

## Success Metrics

### Recruitment
- ✓ 5-10 partners recruited by end Month 1
- ✓ Partners from 3+ different industries
- ✓ Mix of company sizes

### Engagement
- ✓ >80% attendance at weekly 1:1s
- ✓ Written feedback from all partners
- ✓ Active Slack engagement

### Learning
- ✓ Document 20+ feature requests
- ✓ Identify top 3 pain points
- ✓ Test 5+ hypotheses about usage

### Conversion
- ✓ Convert 3+ partners to paying customers
- ✓ Collect testimonials/quotes from all partners
- ✓ 2-3 case studies written

### Transition
- ✓ Clear roadmap for Phase 2 based on feedback
- ✓ Product-market fit signals (partners want more)

---

## Logistics

### Time Commitment
- **Per week**: ~2-3 hours (syncs + Slack)
- **Per month**: ~4 hours (planning, retrospectives)
- **Total**: ~10 hours/week for founder/PM

### Tools
- **Communication**: Slack (for async updates)
- **Calls**: Zoom (recorded with permission)
- **Feedback**: Google Form (quantitative) + Slack (qualitative)
- **Tracking**: Notion/GitHub (manage feedback pipeline)

### Compensation
- **Free access** to AverisOS during Phase 1
- **Special pricing** if they convert to customers (30-50% discount)
- **Public recognition** (logo on website)
- **Earlier access** to new features

---

## Transition to Phase 2 (Public Launch)

At end of Phase 1:
1. **Launch to public**: Make available to anyone
2. **Design partners graduate**: Move to normal customers
3. **Keep relationships**: They're now advocates
4. **Learn from scale**: How does product work with 100+ users?
